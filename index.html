Common Questions

Quick Answers

How do you qualify leads before sending them?⌄

Every lead is sourced from targeted data lists and converted with their explicit consent. We confirm interest and intent through a qualification process — so by the time a lead reaches you, they're expecting contact from a specialist in your field.

Is there a minimum order or lock-in contract?⌄

No contracts, no lock-ins, no minimum commitments. You buy leads as you need them. Most clients start with a batch of 10–20 to validate quality, then scale up.

How fast will I receive my first leads?⌄

Typically 48–72 hours after onboarding. Once your criteria are confirmed, leads start flowing immediately — no long runway.

Are your leads DNCR and Privacy Act compliant?⌄

Yes. We operate under a DNCR Type B licence and all data is handled in compliance with the Privacy Act 1988. All leads are consent-based — prospects have explicitly agreed to be contacted.
